# Interview story bank

Collect specific, truthful examples from work, study, volunteering, or personal projects.
Use your own words; the notes are memory prompts, not a script. Keep confidential employer
information and other people's private details out of your examples.

## Example 1

- Short title:
- Situation: What was happening? Add only the context needed to understand the example.
- Task: What were you responsible for?
- Action: What did you personally do? What options did you consider and why?
- Result: What changed? Use a measure only if you can verify it.
- Reflection: What did you learn, or what would you change next time?

## Quick practice check

- Can I explain my own contribution clearly?
- Is the result accurate and specific without overstating it?
- Have I removed confidential or identifying details?
- Can I tell the story naturally in about one to two minutes?
- What follow-up question might someone reasonably ask?

Use these prompts to prepare honest examples in your own words. They do not guarantee an
interview or job outcome.
